In this build

    
    
      
  • After check-in you choose one exercise: paced breathing, calm place, or grounding in your real room.
    • 
      
  • Calm place uses your cue word only. Bilateral sets inside it stay locked until the therapist's walkthrough.
    • 
      
  • Engine test: up to three slow sets of light and tone on their own, to check timing and comfort. Testing profile only.
    • 
      
  • B or Y at any time: light and sound stop, the scene fades to your real room, and the grounding steps start.
    • 
      
  • Flagged sessions ask whether the stop was real or a test. A real one pauses the app for 4 hours.
    • 
      
  • The crisis card shows sample therapist details. The 988 and 911 lines are real.
    • 
      
  • The lakeshore surroundings are painted as a stand-in until a licensed 360° photo is added.
    • 
    

    
Wording on every screen is a draft until the therapist approves the scripts.
