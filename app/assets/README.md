# Lakeshore photo

The lakeshore scene shows painted surroundings until a photograph is added here.

To replace them, add one file to this folder:

- Name: `lakeshore.jpg`
- Kind: a 360° photo in equirectangular layout (twice as wide as it is tall)
- Size: 4096 × 2048 pixels. The headset spike showed that larger photos look no sharper.
- Subject: a calm lake at dusk, with the horizon level and at the middle of the image, and no people
- Licence: the photo must be licensed for reuse (CC0, for example), or be one you took yourself

The view straight ahead of the seat is three quarters of the way across the image from the left.
The app checks for the file when the page loads and says on the page which one it is using.
