Headset spike

Seven technical checks for the VR stabilization app. There is no exercise content on this page.

1. Run the in-headset checks

  1. Sit down with both controllers in your hands.
  2. Press Start. A panel in front of you gives each instruction.
  3. Trigger means yes, grip means no, A or X repeats. It takes about five minutes.
